How Trinqa manages your portfolio

One portfolio. Liquidity, growth, and money movement coordinated around your intent.

Put idle money to work

Eligible idle funds can be allocated to yield strategies that match your risk, time horizon, and liquidity needs, so your portfolio can keep working until you need the money.

Keep liquidity ready

A portion of your portfolio stays ready to use. When more liquidity is needed, Trinqa can make only the required amount available instead of unwinding everything.

Move only what you need

When it’s time to pay, send, or withdraw, Trinqa coordinates the required liquidity and supported route in the background.

Local money in. Local money out.

Supported local rails can bring money into your portfolio and move it back out when needed, while the infrastructure stays behind the experience.
