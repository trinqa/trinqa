# Trinqa FAQ

## What is Trinqa?

Trinqa is a self-driving portfolio. It coordinates ready-to-use liquidity, eligible yield strategies, and supported money movement around your risk, time horizon, and liquidity needs.

## Is Trinqa a crypto wallet?

No. Trinqa is designed around your portfolio, not around holding or managing crypto assets. Blockchain and financial protocols can be used underneath the product as infrastructure, while the user experience focuses on portfolio value, liquidity, growth, and intent.

## How does Trinqa manage my portfolio?

You choose your risk preference, time horizon, and liquidity needs. Trinqa uses those constraints to determine which supported strategies are eligible and how much of the portfolio should remain ready to use.

## Can I still use my money while part of it is growing?

Trinqa keeps a ready-to-use portion of the portfolio available. When additional liquidity is required, supported strategies can be unwound only as needed rather than treating the entire portfolio as one locked position.

## How does Trinqa choose a strategy?

Trinqa evaluates supported strategies against your risk preference, time horizon, liquidity needs, and strategy characteristics. The goal is not simply to chase the highest advertised yield.

## How do local deposits and withdrawals work?

Supported local payment rails can be used to move money into or out of the portfolio. The underlying rails are infrastructure; they are not the product experience.

## Do I need to understand crypto or DeFi?

No. Trinqa is designed so users can interact with portfolio goals rather than protocols, tokens, vaults, routes, or blockchain mechanics.

## Are returns guaranteed?

No. Returns are not guaranteed. Yield can change and strategies have different risk and liquidity characteristics. Trinqa is designed to manage strategy eligibility around user preferences, not to promise a fixed return.

## What infrastructure does Trinqa use?

Trinqa is being built on Stellar and can use supported financial and DeFi infrastructure behind the scenes for settlement, strategy access, and money movement.

## Is Trinqa available today?

Trinqa is on a waitlist and is not a full public launch. Join the waitlist for early access. A launch date has not been announced.

## What is Ready to use?

Ready to use is the portion of the portfolio kept available for immediate supported actions.

## What is Growing?

Growing is eligible funds currently allocated to supported yield strategies. Returns are not guaranteed.

## How are risk and time horizon used?

Risk preference, time horizon, and liquidity needs determine which supported strategies are eligible and how much of the portfolio should remain ready to use.
